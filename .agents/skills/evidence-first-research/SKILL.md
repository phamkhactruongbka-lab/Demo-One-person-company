# Produce a traceable research brief

1. Translate the question into claims to verify and define region, time range, audience, and decision context.
2. Prefer primary sources: official documentation, filings, product pages, datasets, and direct statements. Use independent secondary sources for context.
3. Record each source URL, publisher, publication/update date when available, access date, supported claim, and relevant limitation.
4. Separate verified facts, inference, estimates, and unanswered questions. Cite the source next to the claim it supports.
5. Summarize the decision implication, confidence, and what evidence would change the conclusion.

Do not fill gaps with invented numbers or present old information as current. For measurements from a private account or system, ask for an authorized read-back or mark the value unavailable.
