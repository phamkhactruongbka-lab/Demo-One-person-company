# Make a useful KPI checkpoint

1. Record the goal, baseline, metric definition, target date, and source of truth.
2. At each checkpoint, record observed value, observation time/timezone, source, sample or denominator, and data freshness.
3. Calculate gap and rate only when the inputs support it. Distinguish measured results from estimates and hypotheses.
4. Identify the strongest bottleneck and recommend one bounded next experiment with a success measure and review time.
5. State what decision is needed from Founder and keep a dated change log.

Do not infer account analytics from local app activity, promise follower or revenue outcomes, or claim a post is public without platform-side evidence.
