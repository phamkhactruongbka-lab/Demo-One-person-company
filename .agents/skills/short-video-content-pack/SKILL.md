# Prepare the video handoff

1. State audience, purpose, platform, duration, aspect ratio, mood, and any brand constraints. Label assumptions.
2. Lead with a clear 1–3 second hook. Draft a scene-by-scene script with timing, visuals, on-screen text, audio, and transition notes.
3. Supply an editable caption, natural CTA, focused hashtags, thumbnail/cover idea, and file/export checklist.
4. Flag rights, sponsorship/disclosure, safety, and AI-generated labeling requirements when applicable.
5. Include the asset path or a clear missing-asset list, then send the package to Kakashi for review.

Keep material original and editable. Treat platform statistics and trend claims as research items, not guarantees. Do not upload or publish; public release requires Founder approval and a supported, authorized platform workflow.
